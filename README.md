## About me

I am a Ph.D. student in the [Department of Civil Engineering, Graduate School of Engineering, The University of Tokyo](https://www.civil.t.u-tokyo.ac.jp/en/), supervised by [Prof. Eiji Hato](https://www.bin.t.u-tokyo.ac.jp/). My research seeks to understand and control interactions among human-driven and automated vehicles while connecting mobility systems with the resilience of cities and energy networks.

My current research spans:

- **Game-informed trajectory prediction and control** — differential games, Nash-equilibrium constraints, interaction-aware prediction, and equilibrium-embedded predictive control.
- **Physics-informed mobility learning** — physics-informed neural networks, graph learning, vehicle dynamics, and interpretable surrogate models.
- **Vision-driven traffic modelling** — map-less car-following models, trajectory data, traffic-flow stability, and mixed traffic.
- **Mobility-energy resilience** — electric vehicles, vehicle-to-grid (V2G) coordination, blackout risk, and resilient urban systems.
- **Travel behaviour modelling** — activity-based analysis, discrete-choice models, and computational tools for mobility research.
