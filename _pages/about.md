  <section id="research" class="research-statement" aria-labelledby="research-title">
    <header class="research-section-heading">
      <p>01 <span aria-hidden="true">/</span> Research question</p>
      <h2 id="research-title">How can connected and autonomous vehicles make sound decisions when every vehicle affects the others?</h2>
    </header>
    <div class="research-statement__body">
      <p>Vehicle trajectories are not isolated forecasts. They emerge from continuous strategic interaction. I study models that expose this interaction rather than hiding it inside a black box, then translate those models into prediction and control methods.</p>
    </div>
  </section>
